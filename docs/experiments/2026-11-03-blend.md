# Blend of the small signals: positive at a sportsbook's close, strongest on the ATP main tour

Date: 2026-11-03. Script: `research_tests/blend/run_blend.py`; output: `research_tests/blend/results.json`.

## Method (pre-registered in the script, after the audit)

Inputs: age gap, qualifier (ATP/WTA main), top-30 pre-Slam week, end of season (ranked 31-90 after
October 20), jet lag. Each is A minus B and public before the match. Logistic on logit(close) plus
the inputs (L2), walk-forward by year from 2016, test 2022-2025, 84,956 matches. The close is the
Kaggle data's "Betfair" sportsbook price, with no commission.

**The inputs were chosen by exploring 2016-2025**, end of season in particular, whose 2022-25 money
was already known. So 2022-2025 is a clean test only of the fitted weights, not of the choice of
inputs. This is a candidate for a registered forward test, not a finding.

All checks pass: swap-source, planted edge (−0.0024 found, −0.0024 expected), market null, known
answers. No alarms.

bookmaker with another's close (line shopping), which this project does not count as an edge.
