# Gate 1 gaps: Pinnacle calibration, Elo information test, and a de-vig correction

Run 2026-10-24 on 2026 fixtures before the seal. Code: `research_tests/gate1/` on the research
branch `claude/clever-fermi-hv34lk`; QA checks pass.
Fixtures: 10,716 with a non-void official winner (`v5_market_results`). 9,621 have a Pinnacle
close.

## Gap 1: Pinnacle calibration
- **By checkpoint** (2,384 fixtures priced at every checkpoint), log loss:

  | Open | t-24h | t-6h | t-1h | Close |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.5858 | 0.5823 | 0.5827 | 0.5823 | 0.5824 |

  The market learns between the open and the day before; almost nothing after.
- **The de-vig method matters.** Pinnacle's close:

  | Margin removal | Log loss | Calibration slope (1 = calibrated) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Multiplicative | 0.58253 | 1.151 |
  | Additive (λ 1) | 0.58159 | 1.096 |
  | Measured λ 1.5 (`v5_devig.BOOK_LAM`, measured 2026-10-16) | 0.58119 | 1.064 |

  The multiplicative split loads too much margin on favourites. Most of the apparent
  favourite-longshot "mispricing" is that, as `v5_devig.py` already documents.
- **By segment, multiplicative slope** (higher = more favourite-longshot):
  - ATP 1.07, WTA 1.08 (both ranges include 1);
  - Challenger 1.20 (1.13, 1.29);
  - WTA125 1.40 (1.17, 1.79), n 460;
  - Clay 1.16, Hard 1.07, Grass 1.11.

  Lower tiers show the strongest bias, so a single λ per book may under-correct Challenger and
  WTA125.
- **Backing the Pinnacle-close favourite at real prices:**
  - Pinnacle: −4.4% at 50-60%, then −0.9%, −0.2%, +0.3%, +0.7% for the higher bands (none above zero);
  - BetRivers: −7.5% to −0.1%;
  - Bet365: too few results.

  The bias exists, and the margin absorbs it.

## Gap 2: does the production Elo add information beyond Pinnacle?
Fit: `logit p = a·logit p_Pinnacle close + b·logit p_Elo`. Production-parameter Elo, both
players with at least 18 rated matches (n 7,002).
- **All fixtures:** b = −0.04 (−0.16, +0.08).
- **Chronological check** (fit before June, test June to the seal): log-loss gain +0.0004
  (−0.0010, +0.0019).
- **By segment:** b is never reliably above zero (ATP +0.06, Challenger +0.01, WTA −0.21, Clay
  +0.02, Hard +0.08, qualifying +0.29 with n 434 and a range from −0.23 to +0.86).

**The production Elo adds nothing beyond Pinnacle's close in any segment.**

## Correction to earlier results (de-vig)
Earlier research compared models with multiplicatively de-vigged prices. That makes the market
look about 0.001 worse than it is.

| Earlier claim | Against the multiplicative benchmark | Against a calibrated benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Ensemble vs the four-book consensus close, Kaggle 2017-25 | +0.0013 (0.0009, 0.0018) | +0.0007 (0.0003, 0.0011); λ 1.5 chosen on 2014-16 only; positive 8 of 9 years |
| Ensemble vs Pinnacle close, 2026 | +0.0009 (−0.0002, +0.0020) | **+0.0001 (−0.0009, +0.0011)** |
| Ensemble vs Pinnacle open, 2026 (Pinnacle as base) | n/a | −0.0003 (−0.0026, +0.0017) |

The model's one consistent adjustment (about +1 point to every favourite) is largely the de-vig
correction. **No model of ours beats Pinnacle's close once the margin is removed correctly.**
From here on, every market benchmark uses `v5_devig` with each book's measured λ.
