# Heat and wind matched to the player: no effect beyond the price; a small general age bias

Date: 2026-11-03. Research, 2016-2025 odds. Scripts: `research_tests/weather/fetch_meteostat.py`
(nearest Meteostat station with hourly data; 357 of 392 locations within 60 km, median 12 km;
`research_tests/weather/stations.csv`) and `research_tests/weather/run_weather.py`; output:
`research_tests/weather/results.json`. Open-Meteo's archive refused the volume (its daily limit
counts request size), so the weather comes from Meteostat's free bulk files.

## Method (pre-registered in the script)

Conditions at the match's start hour at the tournament's location, outdoor matches only. The
history file's indoor flag covers ATP main and part of the Challengers, so 22,070 matches (17,787
ATP, 4,283 Challenger).

- **Hot:** feels-like temperature (NOAA heat index) of 32 C or more. 9.3% of matches.
- **Windy:** wind of 25 km/h or more. 10.0%.

Interactions: heat × age gap, heat × height gap, wind × gap in ace rate (ace rate from earlier
events only, shrunk). All checks pass: swap-source, planted edge (−0.0025 found, −0.0023 expected),
market null, known answers. A first run lost the ace rates (a player could appear twice for one
event id, so the lookup returned a list); this was fixed before reading the results.

## Results

- **PRIMARY:** the three interactions on top of Betfair's close, 2022-2025: −0.00001 log loss
  (−0.00027 to +0.00025). Nothing.
- **Older player (5+ years) in heat:** −1.9 pts (−5.1 to +1.2), the same as the control (older
  player in mild conditions: −1.6 pts, −2.6 to −0.6). The heat adds nothing.
- **Taller player (10+ cm) in heat:** +1.3 pts (−2.0 to +4.7).
- **Bigger server in wind:** −2.1 pts (−4.9 to +0.8), the expected direction but not significant.
  Its money test (2016-21 was −2.5) on 2022-2025: −0.8% at Betfair close (−12% to +11%).

## What it means

Conditions matched to player type add nothing the price misses. The control turned up a small
general age bias: a player 5+ years older does 1.6 points worse than the close in any conditions
(2016-21 −2.1). It is too small to beat the margin on its own (backing the younger player in
2022-25: −3.1% at Betfair close), but it is a candidate input for a blended model.
