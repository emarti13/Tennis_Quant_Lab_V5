# Edge search, 2026-10-20: three test families (research)

Run by three parallel agents on data before the sealed period (2026-10-13), chronological
splits, fees included, ranges resampled by fixture or match. Scripts and full results here;
they read caches from the session scratchpad (rebuild from the repo's market_history and
match files).

| Family | Script | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Execution books (BetRivers, DraftKings, Bet365) vs Pinnacle fair, moneyline/totals/spreads, checkpoints t24h..close (about 400 cells) | `books/run_books.py` | No cell credibly positive; first-half winners turn negative in the second half; totals "edges" significantly negative. One spread cell (t12h, EV>2%, n=23, +57%) is a chance-level hypothesis, not a finding. Kalshi vs Pinnacle untestable before the seal (no overlapping dates). Pinnacle's close beats its open by 0.0055 log loss; almost nothing arrives after t24h. |
| ML on top of Pinnacle (LightGBM, L2 logistic; Elo, fatigue, serve/return, pace, book gaps, line moves, totals/spread lines) at t12h/t6h/t1h | `ml/run_ml.py` | No model beats Pinnacle at the decision time or at the close (LightGBM stops after 1-6 rounds). Positive betting cells have negative CLV (luck); CLV-positive "stale soft book" bets lost money on small samples. |
| Kalshi in-play: LightGBM/logistic on price paths, simple rules (fade favourites, momentum, reversal, take-profit), maker variant | `inplay/run_inplay.py` | Every model worse than Kalshi's mid; every taker rule loses after fees (momentum held: about -3.7%, the fee). Fading 70-90c favourites, one entry per match: -11% (ATP/WTA -7%). The per-sample calibration result of docs/experiments/2026-10-20-inplay-calibration.md does not survive as a tradeable rule. Data: Aug 10-Sep 2 only. |
| Feature mining (LightGBM and logistic, about 9 feature families, leak-free event ordering with exact Elo parity), train 2012-21, test 2023-25 and 2026 | `features/build_features.py`, `features/run_features.py` | Beats Elo by 0.0145 (2023-25) and 0.0137 (2026) log loss, ranges clear of zero; schedule/context (age, home country, recent workload, weeks since last event) is the one irreplaceable family; head-to-head adds nothing. Still worse than Pinnacle's open on 2026 (+0.008; about level on ATP); on top of the open not significant; the close absorbs about 15% of the model's disagreement (open-to-close move toward the model +1.0 point, +1.6 where it differs by 5+). A seed leak in historical qualifying files was found and removed. |
| Deep sequence model (GRU in numpy, PyTorch download host refused by the proxy) over each player's last 30 matches | `deep/run_deep.py` | Beats Elo by 0.015 / 0.0125, the same as a LightGBM on simple averages (sequence adds nothing); nothing on top of Pinnacle. WTA 2026 worse than Elo (2026 WTA rank/height fields look different; not investigated). |
| Feature model and a blend with Pinnacle's open, betting execution books at their open and t24h/t12h/t6h prices (192 cells), 2026 | `early_books/run_early_books.py`, `early_books/check_open_timing.py` | The model alone: negative closing-line value at every checkpoint. The blend at DraftKings' opener looked strong (CLV +5%, n=437) but it is a look-ahead: DraftKings opens a median 2 hours before Pinnacle, so Pinnacle's open was not yet known in 65% of cases; where it was known, CLV is −7%. A few t24h cells with positive returns (n=70-140) are chance-level among 147 cells and have CLV ranges including zero. No edge. |
| First-server effect (Mohammadi 2026, arXiv 2605.04867), 2026-10-21: server inferred from scores and service-game counts (45% of 182k matches), exact iid model under the same selection, pre-match strength only, 2026 Pinnacle/BetRivers/DraftKings/Bet365 totals and handicaps | `first_server/build_fs_table.py`, `first_server/build_market_table.py`, `first_server/run_fs.py` | The paper's Tables 2 and 4 are mostly a selection artifact (a fair-toss iid model reproduces the gaps). The effect itself is real and about the predicted size (totals slope 0.84 ± 0.05, margin 1.22 ± 0.02, placebo ≈ 0). Favourites serve first only slightly more (75% favourite: 53%), so a strength-weighted prior adds nothing to totals (log loss vs Pinnacle unchanged). Pinnacle and BetRivers move part of the shift into the close after the toss (Pinnacle totals 58%, handicaps 23%); the paper's "never updated" is wrong for them. Post-toss handicaps: model value about +3% on about 20% of fixtures, not measurable on past data without the server for every match. Unresolved; needs a forward test with a live server feed. Write-up: `docs/experiments/2026-10-21-first-server-effect.md`. |
| Kaggle odds (alimoh89, 2014-2025; OddsPortal-style, winner-first, no Pinnacle; handicaps unusable), 2026-10-21: LightGBM / logistic / per-tour / blend / ensemble on top of the soft-book consensus, rolling yearly tests 2017-25, ~2,800-cell segment search, executable-price betting, 2026 re-check vs Pinnacle and execution books | `kaggle_odds/join_odds.py`, `kaggle_odds/run_market_models.py`, `kaggle_odds/post_bets.py`, `kaggle_odds/run_2026_check.py`, `kaggle_odds/check_dk_open.py` | Found and fixed a leak in `features/build_features.py` (home country chosen over match rows, so the winning country became home: 54.4% vs 50.7%); the 2026-10-20 feature study's "home country" conclusion needs a re-run. After the fix: ensemble beats the consensus close by 0.0013 log loss every year, Pinnacle 2026 by 0.0009 (ns). Profitable only at Bet365 (+2.4 to +4.3%, mostly 2022-25, untestable in 2026) and, in 2026, at DraftKings' opener (timing-safe, EV > 5%: +8% ROI, closing-line value +2.3%, decaying Jan-May to Jun-Oct). Both unresolved; forward paper test proposed. Write-up: `docs/experiments/2026-10-21-kaggle-odds-edge-search.md`. |
| In-play point model checked on charted points (Match Charting Project, CC BY-NC-SA, raw data not committed), 2026-10-25: exact iid model from the score, serve rates from production Elo, optional in-match updating; train 2011-19, test 2020-26 (5,018 matches, 777k points) | `inplay_charting/build_points.py`, `inplay_charting/run_calibration.py`, `inplay_charting/point_model.py` (+ tests) | Point model calibrated (slope 1.02; every favourite band within about 1 point); in-match updating (k = 200) improves log loss by 0.0075 and removes the apparent momentum effects (after a break or a set, fixed-rate model off by 2 to 5 points; updating model within ranges including zero). Calibrated in the 70-90% bands where Kalshi looked overpriced, but on different matches; no timestamps, so no point-level Kalshi test. A lead for the pre-registered in-play H1/H2 and for a shadow test with a licensed live feed. Write-up: `docs/experiments/2026-10-25-inplay-point-model-charting.md`. |
| Kalshi priced against Pinnacle's no-vig line, pre-registered (2026-10-30, sealed 13-21 Oct, 354 fixtures): T = take Kalshi's ask when 2c+ below Pinnacle after fee; M = quote Kalshi at Pinnacle fair ± 3c/5c, cancel at start or when Kalshi is 10c+ away | `kalshi_pinnacle/run_take.py`, `kalshi_pinnacle/run_make.py` | Kalshi mid sits 0.5c from Pinnacle's fair (median). T fails: 2 signals in 354 fixtures. M passes: +1.1c/contract (0.5, 1.7) on 10,160 contracts at 3c, +3.6c (2.6, 4.5) on 3,011 at 5c, marked at Pinnacle's close after the maker fee; positive every day; mostly Challenger and WTA. Small (about $14/day at 100 lots), one 8-day window, held-to-result negative (noise). Next: forward paper run with live Pinnacle. Write-up: `docs/experiments/2026-10-30-kalshi-vs-pinnacle-results.md`. |
