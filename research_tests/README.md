# Edge search, 2026-10-20: three test families (research)

Run by three parallel agents on data before the sealed period (2026-10-13), chronological
splits, fees included, ranges resampled by fixture or match. Scripts and full results here;
they read caches from the session scratchpad (rebuild from the repo's market_history and
match files).

| Family | Script | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Execution books (BetRivers, DraftKings, Bet365) vs Pinnacle fair, moneyline/totals/spreads, checkpoints t24h..close (about 400 cells) | `books/run_books.py` | No cell credibly positive; first-half winners turn negative in the second half; totals "edges" significantly negative. One spread cell (t12h, EV>2%, n=23, +57%) is a chance-level hypothesis, not a finding. Kalshi vs Pinnacle untestable before the seal (no overlapping dates). Pinnacle's close beats its open by 0.0055 log loss; almost nothing arrives after t24h. |
| ML on top of Pinnacle (LightGBM, L2 logistic; Elo, fatigue, serve/return, pace, book gaps, line moves, totals/spread lines) at t12h/t6h/t1h | `ml/run_ml.py` | No model beats Pinnacle at the decision time or at the close (LightGBM stops after 1-6 rounds). Positive betting cells have negative CLV (luck); CLV-positive "stale soft book" bets lost money on small samples. |
| Kalshi in-play: LightGBM/logistic on price paths, simple rules (fade favourites, momentum, reversal, take-profit), maker variant | `inplay/run_inplay.py` | Every model worse than Kalshi's mid; every taker rule loses after fees (momentum held: about -3.7%, the fee). Fading 70-90c favourites, one entry per match: -11% (ATP/WTA -7%). The per-sample calibration result of docs/experiments/2026-10-20-inplay-calibration.md does not survive as a tradeable rule. Data: Aug 10-Sep 2 only. |
| Feature mining (LightGBM and logistic, about 9 feature families, leak-free event ordering with exact Elo parity), train 2012-21, test 2023-25 and 2026 | `features/build_features.py`, `features/run_features.py` | Beats Elo by 0.0145 (2023-25) and 0.0137 (2026) log loss, ranges clear of zero; schedule/context (age, home country, recent workload, weeks since last event) is the one irreplaceable family; head-to-head adds nothing. Still worse than Pinnacle's open on 2026 (+0.008; about level on ATP); on top of the open not significant; the close absorbs about 15% of the model's disagreement (open-to-close move toward the model +1.0 point, +1.6 where it differs by 5+). A seed leak in historical qualifying files was found and removed. |
| Deep sequence model (GRU in numpy, PyTorch download host refused by the proxy) over each player's last 30 matches | `deep/run_deep.py` | Beats Elo by 0.015 / 0.0125, the same as a LightGBM on simple averages (sequence adds nothing); nothing on top of Pinnacle. WTA 2026 worse than Elo (2026 WTA rank/height fields look different; not investigated). |
| Feature model and a blend with Pinnacle's open, betting execution books at their open and t24h/t12h/t6h prices (192 cells), 2026 | `early_books/run_early_books.py`, `early_books/check_open_timing.py` | The model alone: negative closing-line value at every checkpoint. The blend at DraftKings' opener looked strong (CLV +5%, n=437) but it is a look-ahead: DraftKings opens a median 2 hours before Pinnacle, so Pinnacle's open was not yet known in 65% of cases; where it was known, CLV is −7%. A few t24h cells with positive returns (n=70-140) are chance-level among 147 cells and have CLV ranges including zero. No edge. |
| Kaggle odds dataset (alimoh89, 300,059 matches 2014-2025 incl. ITF; bet365, Betfair, Ladbrokes, Unibet open and close; no Pinnacle) joined to the warehouse | `kaggle_join/join_odds.py`, `kaggle_join/report.json` | 106,767 rows matched one-to-one (ATP main 98.6%, Challenger 83%, WTA main 79%; ITF not in the warehouse); scores agree on 99.9%. player_a is always the winner in the source: re-orient before modelling. |
| Market-structure search on the odds alone (1,899 rules; choose 2014-21, confirm 2022-23, test 2024-25) | `kaggle_market/run_market.py` | No executable edge. The one strong pattern (a LightGBM on bet365/Ladbrokes/Unibet openings, betting bet365's open: +5.1% in 2022-23, +10.8% in 2024-25) earns only on openers bet365 later corrected (openers that stood: −2.7%/−4.4%); opening timestamps are unknown, so it is an upper bound needing live timestamped quotes. Long shots heavily overpriced everywhere (ITF worst); totals lines calibrated; handicap signs unusable. |
